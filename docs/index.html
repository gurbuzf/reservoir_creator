See the reservoir behind any line across a valley

Draw a dam line, pick a DEM, press one button. Get the stored volume, water level, surface area, maximum depth and the elevation–area–volume curves.

Get started How it works GitHub

Three steps

1

Draw the dam line

Click across the valley, or use a line layer.

2

Choose the DEM

A layer in your project, or a global 30 m DEM downloaded for you.

3

Create reservoir

Results, charts and map layers in seconds.

What you get

Full resolution

Computed on the DEM's own cells; a fast mode only if you ask.

Design limits

Optional maximum water level (m a.s.l.) or maximum depth.

Connected water only

Closed hollows below the level are not counted.

Engineering charts

Elevation–area–volume curves, profile and table.

Global DEMs

GEDTM30 or Copernicus, fetched in cached tiles.
