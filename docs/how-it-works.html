How it works

From a line to a reservoir

Everything comes from the DEM.

Four ideas

1 · The level

Above the lower line end, water flows around it.

h = min(zends, your limit)

2 · Connected water

Only cells reached from the line count.

sj = max(zj, si) ≤ h

3 · Area

Flooded cells times cell area.

A(h) = N(h) · Δa

4 · Volume

Water depth over every flooded cell.

V(h) = Σ (h − zi) · Δa
