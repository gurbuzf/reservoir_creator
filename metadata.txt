about=Draw a dam axis across a valley (or pick a line layer) and Reservoir Creator computes the reservoir it would impound directly from a digital elevation model - no contour lines needed. A priority flood from the upstream face of the dam gives the exact elevation-area-capacity relationship, the maximum impoundable level (where water would bypass the dam or spill over a saddle), the water surface and water-depth grid at any level, and planning indicators: dam height, crest length, indicative embankment fill, storage-to-fill ratio, mean and maximum depth, reservoir length, shoreline development, dead/live storage and, with a mean inflow, residence time and Brune sediment trap efficiency. Elevation data can come from a project layer or be downloaded on the fly for the dam area only: GEDTM30 (global bare-earth DTM, OpenGeoHub, CC-BY 4.0) or Copernicus GLO-30. Results are shown in a dockable panel with interactive charts and can be exported to GeoPackage, GeoTIFF and CSV. Requires matplotlib (bundled with QGIS on Windows and macOS).
