about=Draw a line across a valley (or pick a line layer). The water level (m above mean sea level) is the ground height at the lower end of the line, optionally limited by a design maximum water level or maximum depth, and the reservoir is everything behind the line that is connected to it and below that level, computed from the DEM at full resolution. The panel shows the stored volume, water level, surface area and maximum depth, the elevation-area-volume curves, the ground profile along the line and a table, and draws the reservoir on the map. The DEM can be a project layer or downloaded (GEDTM30 bare-earth DTM or Copernicus GLO-30) in cached tiles for the area the reservoir needs. Results can be added to the map or exported (GeoPackage, GeoTIFF, CSV, PNG). English and Turkish interface, light and dark theme. Requires matplotlib (bundled with QGIS on Windows and macOS).
