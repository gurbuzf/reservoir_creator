about=Draw a line across a valley (or pick a line layer). The water level is the ground height at the lower end of the line, and the reservoir is everything behind the line that is connected to it and below that level, computed from the DEM alone. The panel shows the water level, volume, surface area, maximum depth, the elevation-area-volume curve and the ground profile of the line, and draws the reservoir on the map. The DEM can be a project layer or downloaded for the area around the line only (GEDTM30 bare-earth DTM or Copernicus GLO-30), with cancel at any time. Results can be added to the map or exported (GeoPackage, GeoTIFF, CSV, PNG). Requires matplotlib (bundled with QGIS on Windows and macOS).
